A field guide to agentic AI evaluation

Grade the whole job.
Not just the final answer.

When an AI agent takes over multi-step work - booking things, writing code, talking to customers, driving other software - you can't mark it like a quiz answer. You have to watch the whole shift, start to finish, because a small wrong turn early on quietly snowballs into a confidently wrong result later. Checking only "did it get there in the end" overstates real-world reliability by 20–40%.

05 · Batch vs. online evals

The pre-season fitness test vs. the smoke alarm

Batch (offline) evals are a scheduled inspection. Online evals are a smoke alarm that never sleeps. Production agents need both - and the alarm should feed the next inspection's checklist.
